NeoCaffeine

NeoCaffeine is a research simulator for apnea of prematurity. It estimates each infant's hidden caffeine-response parameters hour by hour from apnea counts, blood caffeine levels when available, and respiratory context, then presents individualized dose suggestions for clinician review.

Research tool only. Not a medical device. Outputs are suggestions for qualified clinical review and must never be used for direct patient dosing.
